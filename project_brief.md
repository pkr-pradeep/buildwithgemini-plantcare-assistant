# My agent: PlantCare Assistant
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts care for their home plants and discover new varieties with a catalog of house plants, watering schedules, and diagnostic guides.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's home plant collection, indoor lighting/humidity conditions, watering history, and experience level.
- Tools: Plant catalog search, care schedule calculator, and diagnostic troubleshooting lookup.
- Catalog/UI: House plant catalog featuring care level, sunlight needs, watering frequency, and toxicity info (rendered as cards and tables).
- Image gen: Generating realistic visuals of healthy house plants, flowers, and visual diagnostic guides.
- Sandbox: Calculating custom watering volume and frequency based on pot dimensions, room temperature, and ambient humidity.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for environmental watering calculations, Cloud Trace for monitoring
